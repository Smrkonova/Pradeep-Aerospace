Aero India 2025

At Aero India 2025, I had the unique opportunity to showcase my handmade aircraft models, intricately crafted with micro-detailing, as part of the exhibition.

These models represent indigenous designs and were developed with a focus on precision, innovation, and educational outreach. Displaying them at one of Asia’s largest aerospace and defence exhibitions was a proud moment, allowing me to contribute to the spirit of 'Aatmanirbhar Bharat' in the aerospace sector.

The exhibition not only provided a platform to connect with defence professionals, students, and enthusiasts but also offered valuable recognition for my efforts in promoting aerospace awareness through detailed, handmade model displays.
