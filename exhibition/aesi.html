AeSI AGM & Vikshit Bharat Conference 2025, DRDO Pune

1. During the AeSI AGM & Vikshit Bharat Conference 2025 held at DRDO Pune, I had the privilege of exhibiting my stall featuring indigenous handmade 2D fighter aircraft models. Shri Kinjarapu Ram Mohan Naidu, Hon’ble Minister of Civil Aviation of India, visited the stall and took a keen interest in the aircraft models on display.

I had the honor of personally explaining the design, development process, and unique indigenous features of each aircraft. The interaction was both encouraging and inspiring, highlighting the importance of innovation and self-reliance in India's aerospace sector.

2. During the AeSI AGM & Vikshit Bharat Conference 2025 held at DRDO Pune, I had the honor of showcasing my stall featuring indigenous handmade 2D fighter aircraft models. The highlight of the event was the visit of the Air Officer Commanding-in-Chief (AOC-in-C), Air Marshal, who took keen interest in the display. I had the privilege of personally explaining the design, concept, and indigenous features of each aircraft model. His engagement and appreciation were truly motivating and served as a recognition of the effort put into promoting self-reliance and innovation in aerospace.

3. During the AeSI AGM & Vikshit Bharat Conference 2025 held at DRDO Pune, I had the privilege of exhibiting my stall featuring indigenous handmade 2D fighter aircraft models.

I was deeply honoured by the visit of Shri Dr. S. Somanath, Chairman of the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), who took a keen interest in the models on display.

I had the opportunity to personally explain the design concepts, development process, and the unique indigenous features of each aircraft. The interaction was both encouraging and inspiring, reinforcing the vital role of innovation, creativity, and self-reliance in advancing India’s aerospace sector.
