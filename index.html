About Me

Born on June 8th, 1990, in Kalaburagi, Karnataka, I come from an agricultural family where my father's teaching profession sparked my passion for science, art, and culture. This unique blend led me from rural roots to aerospace excellence.

Currently serving as Senior Lead Aerospace Engineer at Collins Aerospace (Raytheon Technologies), I previously spent 5 years as Senior Research Scientist at DRDO, contributing to indigenous defense systems like AEW&C and SAR for UAV.

Beyond engineering, I'm a 2D aviation artist creating detailed handmade aircraft models. Since 2011, I've been conducting India's first volunteer aerospace awareness campaign, reaching 8,000+ students across Karnataka government schools through aircraft demonstrations and exhibitions.

My mission is simple: inspire young minds to pursue aerospace innovation through the perfect fusion of technical expertise and artistic expression, contributing to India's Make in India and Atmanirbhar Bharat vision.

Approved Letters

  • 2024

    Acknowledgment of Support for Aerospace & Defence Awareness Campaign

    We acknowledge the receipt of the official communication from the Commissioner’s Office, School Education Department, Nrupatunga Road, Bangalore, regarding the list of government schools approved for the Aerospace and Defence Awareness Campaign by Mr. Pradeep L. Savalkar, Ex-DRDO Research Scientist and Senior Aerospace Software Engineer.

    As part of this initiative, Mr. Savalkar will conduct a special talk on “Aircraft Demonstration Activity” along with an exhibition of indigenous handmade 2D aircraft models, showcasing India’s advancements in aerospace and defence.

    The program will also include interactive sessions designed to engage students and inspire curiosity about aerospace technologies, defence innovations, and indigenous contributions to national progress.

    We sincerely thank the Commissioner’s Office and the School Education Department for their support and look forward to the successful implementation of this campaign for the benefit of students in the listed government schools.

  • 2025

    AeSI OFFICIAL CERTIFICATION

    This is to certify that Mr. Pradeep L. Savalkar is duly authorized by the Aeronautical Society of India (AeSI), Head Office, to conceptualize, coordinate, and represent AeSI in connection with the Aerospace and Defence Awareness Campaign for Government Schools during the academic year 2025–2026.

    As part of this initiative, Mr. Savalkar will:

    Deliver special talks on “Indigenous Aircraft Demonstration Activities”

    Organize exhibitions of handmade 2D indigenous aircraft models with micro-detailing

    Engage with school authorities, students, and communities to promote aerospace education, scientific temper, and national pride

    We request all concerned authorities, institutions, and stakeholders to kindly extend their cooperation and support to Mr. Savalkar for the successful implementation of this educational outreach initiative.

    For and on behalf of

    The Aeronautical Society of India (AeSI)
