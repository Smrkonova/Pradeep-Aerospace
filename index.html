About Me

Born on June 8, 1990, in Kalaburagi, Karnataka, I was inspired by my father’s role as a teacher to pursue science, art, and culture. Today, I serve as Senior Lead Aerospace Engineer at Collins Aerospace and earlier worked at DRDO on key indigenous defense projects like AEW&C and SAR for UAVs.

Since 2018, I have led India’s first volunteer-driven aerospace awareness campaign—Igniting Young Minds—reaching over 23,000 students across Karnataka. Through model exhibitions, demos, and talks, the initiative inspires youth from underserved communities to take pride in India’s aerospace, defense, and space achievements, aligned with Make in India and Atmanirbhar Bharat.

HAL – RUDRA 2D

HAL – RUDRA 2D Aircraft Model with Microdetailing

A completely handmade 2D model of the HAL Rudra, India’s first indigenous armed helicopter developed by Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL). Based on the Advanced Light Helicopter (ALH) Dhruv platform, this artwork captures the Rudra’s combat-ready configuration, including its turret-mounted cannon, rocket pods, anti-tank guided missiles, and advanced sensor systems. Created with intricate micro-detailing, the model reflects the helicopter’s multirole capabilities in battlefield support, reconnaissance, and close air support. Entirely handcrafted, this piece highlights India's growing strength in indigenous rotary-wing combat aviation under the Make in India and Atmanirbhar Bharat initiatives.

Indigenous LCA TEJAS Mk-1A 24″x12″ Hand Made 2D

Indigenous LCA TEJAS Mk-1A 24″x12″ Hand Made 2D Aircraft Model with Microdetailing

A completely handmade 2D model of the indigenous LCA Tejas, a 4.5-generation multirole fighter aircraft. This artwork features nine external hardpoints, a meticulously detailed cockpit system, and an accurate representation of its advanced weapon systems — all captured through precision micro-detailing. Crafted entirely by hand, this model showcases the technological brilliance of India's defense capabilities through the lens of fine aviation art.

Indigenous Airbus C-295 2D

Indigenous Airbus C-295 2D Aircraft Model with Microdetailing

A completely handmade 2D model of the Airbus C295, the new-generation tactical transport aircraft being manufactured in India in collaboration with Tata Advanced Systems. This detailed artwork showcases the aircraft’s high-wing design, rear ramp door, and multi-mission capability — including troop transport, medical evacuation, and cargo delivery. The model also highlights key external features such as its twin turboprop engines, landing gear, and aerodynamic structure, all captured with precision micro-detailing. Fully handcrafted, this piece celebrates India’s leap in aerospace manufacturing and technology under the Make in India and Atmanirbhar Bharat initiatives.

DRDO AEW&C – NETRA – Eye in the Sky 2D

DRDO AEW&C – NETRA – Eye in the Sky 2D Aircraft Model with Microdetailing

A completely handmade 2D model of the DRDO AEW&C NETRA — India’s indigenous Airborne Early Warning and Control (AEW&C) system developed by DRDO and mounted on the Embraer EMB-145 platform. This detailed artwork features the signature dorsal Active Electronically Scanned Array (AESA) radar dome, satellite communication antennas, electronic warfare systems, and mission operator consoles, all represented with precision micro-detailing. Entirely handcrafted, the model pays tribute to India’s self-reliance in surveillance and network-centric warfare capabilities, showcasing the spirit of Atmanirbhar Bharat through aviation art.

Boeing 777-300ER 2D

Boeing 777-300ER 2D Aircraft Model with Microdetailing

A completely handmade 2D model of the Boeing 777-300ER, one of the world’s most advanced and long-range wide-body commercial airliners. This artwork captures the aircraft’s iconic features — including its extended fuselage, raked wingtips, GE90 engines, and signature landing gear configuration — with meticulous micro-detailing. Handcrafted with precision, the model reflects the scale, proportions, and elegance of this engineering marvel, often used by major international airlines for ultra-long-haul operations. This piece showcases the beauty of civil aviation through the lens of fine, indigenous aviation art.

Indigenous TEJAS MK2 21”x11” Hand Made 2D

Indigenous TEJAS MK2 21”x11” Hand Made 2D Aircraft Model with Microdetailing

A completely handmade 2D model of the indigenous LCA Tejas Mk2, India’s advanced 4.5-generation multirole fighter aircraft. This intricate artwork features 13 external hardpoints, a meticulously crafted cockpit, and a highly detailed depiction of its sophisticated weapon systems — all rendered with precision micro-detailing. Entirely handcrafted, the model reflects the technological excellence of India’s aerospace engineering, brought to life through the unique medium of fine aviation art.

Indigenous AMCA Hand Made 2D

Indigenous AMCA Hand Made 2D Aircraft Model with Microdetailing

A completely handmade 2D model of the indigenous Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft (AMCA) — India’s fifth-generation stealth multirole fighter aircraft currently under development. This artwork showcases the AMCA’s distinctive design, including internal weapons bays, stealth geometry, serpentine air intakes, and advanced avionics layout. Featuring precision micro-detailing, the model captures the aircraft’s aerodynamic contours, low-observable features, and mission-specific configurations. Crafted entirely by hand, this piece reflects India’s future in aerospace innovation through the lens of intricate aviation art.

Boeing 737-800NG 2D

Boeing 737-800NG 2D Aircraft Model with Microdetailing

A completely handmade 2D model of the Boeing 737-800 Next Generation (NG) — one of the most successful and widely used narrow-body commercial aircraft in the world. This artwork features the aircraft’s distinctive winglets, twin-engine layout, and elongated fuselage, all rendered with precision micro-detailing. Handcrafted with accuracy and artistic finesse, the model captures the essence of modern commercial aviation, highlighting the aircraft’s role in global connectivity and airline operations. A tribute to engineering excellence, this piece brings the world of civil aviation to life through indigenous aviation art.

    Acknowledgment of Support for Aerospace & Defence Awareness Campaign
